SketchbookAI – Urban Photoreal car models (Sketchfab)

The three vehicle shells are original Sketchfab GLB downloads, not synthetic replacements.
They are visually attached to the stock Sketchbook vehicle physics rig.
Their original authors retain credit, and the Creative Commons Attribution license applies
where designated on the source model pages.
